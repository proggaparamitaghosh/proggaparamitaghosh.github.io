I am Progga Paramita Ghosh, with an interdisciplinary academic background in urban and regional planning, remote sensing, and GIS. My interests center on understanding urban and environmental challenges through geospatial analysis, quantitative research, and evidence-based planning, with particular attention to environmental conditions, spatial disparities, and community well-being.

My academic and professional experience spans environmental research, urban planning, and community development. My work includes air pollution analysis, park accessibility and equity assessment, and transportation safety analysis through a Vision Zero project. I have also contributed to research on urban heat vulnerability and watershed toxicity, as well as community-focused projects involving housing, demographic analysis, and neighborhood development. Across these experiences, I have used GIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, and data visualization to investigate urban challenges and communicate research findings.

This portfolio presents my research, publications, professional experience, and selected projects, including their methods, findings, and outputs.
